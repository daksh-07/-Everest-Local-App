# Research evidence — accessed 28 October 2026

Fresh research below is public documentation, not hands-on competitor app testing. Product examples explain hypotheses; they do not establish Everest conversion effects. Prior broader competitor notes are in `docs/design/competitive-research-2026.md` and `docs/experience-v2-research.md`; their dates and weaker/third-party evidence labels still apply.

| Source | Verified public evidence | Everest interpretation / decision |
|---|---|---|
| [W3C form notifications](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/notifications/) | Describe errors, give correction guidance, and consider focusing the first invalid input | Focus missing locality/country; retain entered data. Applied |
| [W3C form instructions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/instructions/) | Indicate optional/required inputs and provide accessible instructions | Persistent location labels and explicitly optional administrative fields. Applied |
| [WCAG 2.2 target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum) | AA target-size criterion includes size and spacing exceptions | Keep 44–50 px controls rather than treat 24 px as a design goal. Not a whole-app conformance claim |
| [NN/g progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) | Defer advanced/infrequent options while retaining access | Preserve Today → attention → CRM hierarchy; test optional address detail disclosure before further changes |
| [Baymard field marking](https://baymard.com/research-articles/required-optional-form-fields) | Ambiguous required/optional fields cause unnecessary effort and missed inputs in their ecommerce research | Mark field requirements clearly; no transfer of Baymard percentages to Everest |
| [Taskrabbit hiring](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/46260422073755-How-Do-I-Hire-a-Tasker) | Search/category, task location, provider details/rate/reviews, date/time, details and review precede confirmation; provider-side scheduling determines scheduled state | Preserve review/authoritative status; expose useful recovery from empty category results. Do not copy its registration sequence blindly |
| [Jobber Home](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/home-in-the-jobber-app/) | Role-specific Home and up-next/day work | Preserve jobs-first Today rather than bring advanced CRM ahead of work. Fresh source search/extraction; no signed-in app inspection |
| [Fresha calendar](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/calendar) and [appointment creation](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/calendar/260-create-appointments-1) | Calendar exposes appointments, blocked time, availability and scheduling; automated assignment requires an eligible available professional | Future employee shortcuts must respect eligibility/availability, never silently assign. Search-result evidence only |
| [Google Maps search](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/3092445?hl=en) | Public help provides result filtering | Location/search/filter controls should have predictable scopes. Search-result evidence only; no claims about current mobile layout |
| [Airbnb total price](https://news.airbnb.com/total-price-display-is-now-standard-globally) | Official announcement says total price including fees appears in search | Compare meaningful totals where authoritative totals exist; never invent all-in quote prices. Search-result evidence only |

Apple HIG Entering Data and Material 3 Text Fields official URLs were retrieved, but yielded JavaScript-only pages; Apple's Markdown endpoint was unsupported by the search tool. They are **not** counted as verified substantive guidance in this pass. Baymard's old optional-fields URL failed; the accessible current research article above was used instead.
