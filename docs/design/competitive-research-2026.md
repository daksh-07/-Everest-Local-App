# Competitive research — October 2026

Accessed 16 October 2026. Extends `docs/experience-v2-research.md` (12 Oct 2026: Uber, Etsy custom orders, Airbnb, Google Maps, Linear, Shopify, Fresha, WhatsApp, Material and HIG references); this pass does not repeat those.

## Method and limits

- **Direct observation** means the page itself was fetched and read. Only Airtasker's How it works page was read directly in this pass; hipages.com.au refused automated fetch and Thumbtack returned an empty shell.
- **Reported** means the finding comes from a search-engine summary of the linked pages (official help centres, newsrooms, or credible press). Those are labelled as such. Treat them as weaker evidence than direct observation.
- No native or authenticated competitor app was opened. No competitor statistics are reproduced. Interpretations are marked **Everest interpretation**.

## Decisions informed by this research

1. **Location, then intent.** Home's first element is the location control; search and the two intents follow (rows 2, 14).
2. **Navigation-only tabs.** Removed the Home hamburger menu, which re-listed tab destinations (rows 7, 8). Ask Everest moved into the search bar, replacing an inert filters icon.
3. **Attention first.** Activity and Business Today both lead with items that need a decision (rows 1, 10).
4. **Honest live state.** No ETAs, distances or provider counts beyond what `get_everest_live_state` returns (rows 4, 12).
5. **Badges must mean something.** Verified checkmarks carry an accessible "verified" label and remain tied to ABN verification (row 5).
